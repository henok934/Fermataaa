Our Vision & Mission

Busfermata Digital Technology is a visionary, unique, and independent logistics platform established to modernize the travel experience.

By merging high-level technical expertise with deep real-world experience, we are bridging the digital gap in Ethiopia’s travel industry. Our platform is built to resolve the daily challenges of travel—making every journey across the nation faster, safer, and more reliable.
